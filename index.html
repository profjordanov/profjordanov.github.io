Early Life

I was born in the town of Lukovit on October 30, 1995. My mother is Rositsa Pelova Ivanova, and my father is Ivan Yordanov Ivanov. I have an older sister, Desislava Ivanova Dimitrova, who married young and settled in Varna. She has two children, Teodora and Ivan. I completed my secondary education at Aleko Konstantinov High School in Lukovit, where I lived with my parents and grandmother, Tinka Ivanova Pelova. Growing up, we often visited my native village, Lyutadzhik. As a child, I was curious, yet a bit mischievous. Mathematics and computers sparked my interest from an early age, but I also enjoyed playing sports and spending time with friends. I deeply valued my freedom, making the most of every moment. While I didn’t devote excessive time to studying, I always tried to approach it efficiently and purposefully.

In 2014, I was admitted to the University of Economics - Varna after successfully passing the mathematics exam. During my studies, I was highly motivated to pursue growth and secure a position as a programmer. To achieve this, I began studying simultaneously at SoftUni. During the summer seasons of 2015 and 2016, I worked as an animator at the Astera Hotel in the Golden Sands resort. In the fall of 2016, I faced a significant challenge when I underwent surgery for torn cruciate ligaments—marking the first serious test in my life. The COVID-19 pandemic of 2020 brought a profound personal loss, as I lost my father, Ivan. Born on May 18, 1955, he passed away on October 30, 2020.
